# Card games cheat sheet

Scoring and setup for the games we play at the kitchen table. The scorekeepers on the Card Games page do the math for you.

## Five Crowns

**Deck:** two decks shuffled together, 116 cards: five suits (stars, hearts, clubs, spades, diamonds) of 3 to king, plus 6 jokers.

**Rounds:** 11. Round 1 deals 3 cards each, and each round deals one more, up to 13 in round 11.

**Wild cards:** the rank matching the round's deal is wild: 3s in round 1, 4s in round 2, up to kings in round 11. Jokers are always wild.

**Melds:** a *book* is 3+ cards of the same rank. A *run* is 3+ cards in a row in the same suit. When someone goes out, everyone else gets one more turn.

| Left in your hand | Points |
|---|---|
| 3 to 10 | Face value |
| Jack / Queen / King | 11 / 12 / 13 |
| That round's wild rank | 20 |
| Joker | 50 |

**Lowest total after 11 rounds wins.**

## Phase 10

**Deck:** 108 cards: 1 to 12 in four colors (two of each), 8 Wilds, 4 Skips. Deal 10 each.

| Phase | Lay down |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 sets of 3 |
| 2 | 1 set of 3 + 1 run of 4 |
| 3 | 1 set of 4 + 1 run of 4 |
| 4 | 1 run of 7 |
| 5 | 1 run of 8 |
| 6 | 1 run of 9 |
| 7 | 2 sets of 4 |
| 8 | 7 cards of one color |
| 9 | 1 set of 5 + 1 set of 2 |
| 10 | 1 set of 5 + 1 set of 3 |

Miss your phase and you try it again next hand.

| Left in your hand | Points |
|---|---|
| 1 to 9 | 5 each |
| 10 to 12 | 10 each |
| Skip | 15 |
| Wild | 25 |

**The first to finish Phase 10 wins. If two finish in the same hand, the one with fewer points wins.**

## Skip-Bo

**Deck:** 162 cards: 1 to 12 (12 of each) plus 18 Skip-Bo wilds.

**Setup:** stock piles are 30 cards for 2 to 4 players and 20 for 5 or more. Hold 5 cards and refill to 5 at the start of each turn.

**Play:** build piles in the middle start at 1 and climb to 12, then are cleared. You may only play the top card of your stock pile. End your turn by discarding to one of your four discard piles.

**Scoring (house league):** only the hand winner scores, **25 points plus 5 per card left in the other players' stock piles**. First to 500 wins. 250 makes a short night, 1,000 a long one.

## Dealing and shuffling

| Rule of thumb | Why |
|---|---|
| Riffle shuffle 7 times | Fewer riffles leave a 52-card deck measurably non-random |
| Deal one card at a time, left of the dealer first | Stops clumps from the shuffle landing in one hand |
| Rotate the dealer each hand | Keeps any dealing edge fair |
| Count the deck before game night | A missing card quietly changes the odds |
